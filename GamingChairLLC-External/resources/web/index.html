Global

Overview and shared state for the application.

WalkSpeed

WalkSpeed Enabled Toggle custom walk speed for your character.
WalkSpeed Value Set the walk speed (default 16, max 500).
WalkSpeed Keybind Toggle WalkSpeed in game. Backspace clears.

NoClip

NoClip Enabled Walk through walls (re-applies every ~7.5s).
NoClip Keybind Toggle NoClip in game. Backspace clears.

Fly

Fly Enabled Freeze in place and move with WASD, Space and Ctrl. Hold Q to accelerate.
Fly Speed Movement multiplier. 1.0 is the good folder's default.
Boost Power Exponent for the Q boost ramp. 4.0 is the original.
Floor Margin How far above Roblox's kill plane the clamp sits.
Face Camera Turn the character to match where you are looking.
Fly Keybind Toggle Fly in game. Locked while Fling is on. Backspace clears.

Fling

Fling Enabled Tumble on random angular velocity. Turns Fly on automatically.
Fling Speed Spin strength. 1000 is the good folder's default.
Fling Keybind Toggle Fling in game. Backspace clears.
Sweep Enabled Teleport to each other player in turn, then back. Turns Fly on automatically.
Sweep Dwell How long to stay on each player before moving on.
Sweep Keybind Start or stop a Sweep in game. Backspace clears.

Combat

Aim assistance and target handling.

Aim Assist

Aim Assist Enabled Smoothly keep the camera on the nearest other player.
Smoothing Time constant in seconds. Lower snaps faster; 0 is instant.
Strength How much of the eased aim is applied each tick.
Stickiness How much closer a new target must be before the aim switches.
Screen Check Only pick players near the crosshair. Off picks the nearest player anywhere.
Screen Radius How far from the crosshair a target can be, in pixels.
Aim Part Which part of the target the camera points at.
Aim Assist Keybind Toggle Aim Assist in game. Backspace clears.
